About Me

Economics & Statistics graduate with professional certification in Monitoring & Evaluation.

I am a dedicated, results-driven research and data professional with over five years of experience delivering qualitative and quantitative research across Africa and Asia. I have led and supported end-to-end projects, including proposal development, tool design, field coordination, data analysis and reporting, for UN agencies, INGOs and research consultancies.

My methodological foundation spans both qualitative and quantitative research, with particular depth in baseline, mid-term and end-line evaluations. I have worked with health and WASH data systems aligned with national and donor reporting frameworks, and regularly manage several projects at once with diverse, multidisciplinary teams.

In the field I design tools and conduct IDIs, KIIs, FGDs and observations; at the desk I clean, validate and analyse large survey datasets in SPSS, STATA, R, Python and Excel. Whatever the assignment, the goal is the same: rigorous, timely outputs that support data-driven decisions and lasting social impact.
